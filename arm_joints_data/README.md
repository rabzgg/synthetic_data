**TL;DR**
- `original/`: the six real XDK recordings, unchanged. They come from one session (2026-05-05,
  13:38–15:12 UTC, 95 min, 10 Hz, one shared clock).
- `synthetic_full_feed/`: 60 min of synthetic data per joint (seed 42), generated by the
  joint-template engine from the **whole 95-min recording**.
- `synthetic_10min_feed/`: the same engine and seed, but the bank is built from **only the first
  10 min** of the recording.
- `plots/`: real first 60 min (black) vs synthetic (orange), orientation x/y/z and acceleration
  x/y/z. Each plot shows the whole hour plus a 3-min zoom.
- Physics gate: **only joints 1–3 with full feed pass.** The other three sets fail (table below).

## How the data was made
- Joints 1–3 and joints 4–6 use **two separate banks**. Within a group, the three files share
  one cycle schedule, so they move together. **The two groups are not synchronised with each
  other.**
- Full feed: the bank holds every usable 37-s arm cycle of the recording:
  - joints 1–3: 120 of 150 cycles;
  - joints 4–6: 147 of 151 cycles.
- 10-min feed: the first 600 s from the common start of the group (13 of 15 cycles for joints
  1–3, 15 of 16 for joints 4–6). One hour of output therefore repeats those few cycles. The slow
  heading drift of the real data stops after minute 10 (visible in the plots).
- The environment columns (temperature, light, humidity, pressure) come from a config fitted on
  the first 10 min, in both variants.
- Synthetic files use integer-millisecond timestamps from the generator clock. Original files
  keep their ISO timestamps and raw column names (`quat_*`, `accel_*`).

## Physics gate (seed 42; reference = real first 60 min; thresholds derived from data)

| set | gate | failing blocking checks | C2ST (reported only, lower = harder to tell apart) |
|---|---|---|---|
| joints 1–3, full feed | **PASS** | — | 0.68 / 0.67 / 0.71 |
| joints 1–3, 10-min feed | FAIL | mag_world (joint 1) | 0.87 / 0.87 / 0.90 |
| joints 4–6, full feed | FAIL | mag_world (joints 4, 5, 6), copy (joint 5) | 0.76 / 0.65 / 0.74 |
| joints 4–6, 10-min feed | FAIL | mag_world (joints 4, 5), copy (joint 5), omega p99 (joint 6) | 0.90 / 0.90 / 0.90 |

- **Joints 4–6, mag_world.** The magnetometers of joints 4 and 6 show a large disturbance only
  while the arm moves (residual sd 7–14 µT, against ≤ 3.9 µT on joints 1, 2, 3 and 5). The engine
  takes that high-frequency part from another real cycle, so it no longer matches the
  orientation. The orientation and accelerometer columns are not affected.
- **Joint 5, copy.** This check is not informative for joint 5. Real joint-5 data held out from
  the reference already scores 98 % "copy" against the reference, the same as joint 3, which the
  gate already excludes. Joint 5 is not excluded yet; that is a pending decision.
- **10-min feed.** Ten minutes of input do not contain the slow drift and variation of a full
  hour, so the classifier separates it easily (C2ST ≈ 0.9).
