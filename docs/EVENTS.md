# Events and scenarios

_Generated by `python3 cli/events_doc.py` from `configs/events/`, `configs/scenarios/` and `configs/devices/`; do not edit by hand._

An event changes the world (its drivers or the body's motion; the world then derives RH, O2, pressure and the body tracks again) or one device (what its sensors are given, its states, its connection). It never writes a CSV column. Events are data (`configs/events/*.yaml`, format in `core/events/library.py`). A world event is seen by a device when it changes something the device measures, at its mount, by at least that sensor's resolution (tried numerically); a device event when the device has the features it needs and measures what it is given. An event no device of a run can see is refused with the reason. Building blocks are small events meant for composites.

## What the labels say

Each run writes, per device, the events it saw (scope `world` or `device`) and what the device should do in response (scope `expected`):

- **No motion** (device feature `motion_alerts.no_motion`): after `detect_s` without any movement a `no_motion_pre_alarm` to the wearer for `pre_alarm_s`, then a `no_motion_alert`, until the body moves again.
- **Fall** (feature `motion_alerts.fall`): a `fall_pre_alarm` from the moment the wearer lies at rest, for `pre_alarm_s`; getting up within it cancels it, otherwise a `fall_alert` until the wearer gets up.
- **Collapse**: `collapse:gas_release` → `collapse:fall` → `collapse:stir` (moves a little, `no_motion_after_s`) → `collapse:no_motion` (`no_motion_s`) → `collapse:rescue`; the gaps are parameters.
