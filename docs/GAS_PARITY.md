## Why the failures

- **`alarm_cause`**: a physical effect the old engine did not model. A combustible gas above 100 %LEL displaces air: 128 %LEL of methane is 6.4 %vol, so oxygen falls to about 19.5 %vol and the O2 low alarm (<= 19.5) goes off. The old rule only accepts an O2 alarm during an event on O2 itself, so it calls it an alarm without a cause. The new world derives O2 from the other gases (core/world/physics.py); the alarm is right.

- **`bump_simultaneous`**: an old rule tied to the old engine's 10-s steps, not a fault in the new data. The old rule wants every gas of the bump mix to have moved by more than twice its resolution in the very first bump-test row. The new generator applies the test gas at 06:02:00 and logs that same second, so the sensor (t90 30 s) has seen the gas for one second: on the MSA detectors H2S (resolution 1 ppm) reads 2 ppm, not more than 2, and passes the band ten seconds later; the other gases move in the first row. The old engine filtered the response in 10-s steps, so its first row always held 10 s of exposure. The bump is simultaneous; the new gate checks the response with input_response and passes.
